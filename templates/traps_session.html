  • Firewall — allows/blocks by IP, port, protocol (and app, if NGFW). The control that enforces the zones above.
  • WAF — inspects HTTP(S) for app attacks (SQLi, XSS); sits in front of web servers in the DMZ.
  • IDS — detects and alerts on suspicious traffic (out-of-band, a copy of traffic).
  • IPS — detects and blocks inline, dropping malicious packets in real time.
  • TLS — encrypts data in transit; a confidentiality/integrity control, not a traffic-filtering appliance.
